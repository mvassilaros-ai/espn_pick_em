How V11 thinks
1. Cover probability: current consensus market versus the frozen pool line.

3. Accuracy first: the higher cover probability wins unless the sides are close enough for standings-aware leverage.

4. Public leverage: can break close decisions only; the allowable probability sacrifice grows automatically when late and behind.
